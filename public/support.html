Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to the most common SmartMove questions.

Navigate to Book Ticket from the navigation bar. Fill in your passenger details, choose your route and preferred trip, then confirm your booking. You'll receive a booking confirmation instantly with your ticket reference number.
Booking modifications and cancellations must be requested at least 2 hours before the scheduled departure. Contact our support team via Live Chat or email with your ticket reference number and the changes you require.
If SmartMove cancels a trip, you will be notified via the announcements board and, where possible, reassigned to the next available trip on the same route. Check Announcements for real-time operational updates.
Visit the Routes page to browse and search all available routes. You can filter by origin, destination, and view details like distance and base fare. Popular routes are also previewed on the homepage.
Yes. SmartMove's route and trip data is powered by our Oracle RDBMS backend, updated in real time by our operations team. Any schedule changes are immediately reflected on the Routes page and communicated via Announcements.
SmartMove accepts cash payments at the point of travel as well as offline payment logging by administrators. Online card payment integration is on our roadmap. Contact support for payment-related concerns with your booking reference.
Fares are calculated based on the route's base fare as defined in the system. The base fare per route is visible on the Routes page. Additional charges may apply based on trip type or special arrangements.
Passenger travel history is accessible through the SmartMove Admin Portal under the Passengers section. For personal history requests, contact our support team with your registered name and contact number and we'll provide a detailed travel record.
To update your name, phone number, or email address on record, please contact our support team via email or Live Chat. Provide your original registration details and the corrections you need made.
